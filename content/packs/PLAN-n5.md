# N5 course plan: 12 units after the foundation

Each unit is one content pack (`content/packs/n5-uNN.json`) in the textbook order the app teaches: new words → a scene heard without text → practice in new sentences (gaps, replies, sentence building, questions on the scene) → a letter with comprehension tasks. Units follow the grammar sequence of Genki I lessons 1–12 so the app and the book reinforce each other. Situations, sentences and characters are original; nothing is copied from Genki.

Cast (stable personalities): **Yui** (keeps the bookshop, calm and kind), **Ren** (runs the café, friendly, dry jokes), **Hana** (American classmate, cheerful), **佐藤先生 Satō-sensei** (the Japanese teacher, warm but exact), **You** (a Vietnamese university student learning Japanese).

| Unit | Title | Genki I | Grammar | Can-do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n5-u01 | Who's who | L1 | X は Y です, か, の, 何 (なん/なに), time | Introduce yourself with country, year and major; ask the time |
| n5-u02 | At the bookshop | L2 | これ/それ/あれ, この/その/あの, prices, ください | Ask what things are and how much, and buy one |
| n5-u03 | A Sunday film | L3 | ます verbs, に/で/を/へ, ませんか, いいですね | Say what you do at the weekend and invite someone |
| n5-u04 | Where is it? | L4 | あります/います, location words, past ました | Ask where things and people are; say what you did |
| n5-u05 | A trip to Kyoto | L5 | い/な adjectives, past adjectives, 好き/きらい, ましょう | Describe places and things; suggest doing something |
| n5-u06 | May I? | L6 | て-form, てください, てもいいですか, てはいけません | Ask for help and permission politely |
| n5-u07 | Family photos | L7 | ている, describing people, て-joining | Talk about family, where people live and work |
| n5-u08 | Casual talk | L8 | short (plain) forms, と思います, ないでください | Talk casually with friends; give opinions |
| n5-u09 | Last weekend | L9 | past short forms, から (reason), もう/まだ | Tell a friend what you did and why |
| n5-u10 | Winter plans | L10 | より/のほうが/一番, つもり, かかります | Compare things; say plans and how long travel takes |
| n5-u11 | After the holiday | L11 | たい, たり〜たり, ことがある | Say what you want to do and have done |
| n5-u12 | Feeling unwell | L12 | んです, すぎる, ほうがいい, ので | Explain a problem and give advice |

Per unit: 12–16 new words or chunks (particles introduced where first needed), 2 grammar notes, one 5–7 line scene, 7–9 exercises (at least two gaps, one reply, one sentence to build, two or three questions on the scene), one letter of 5–7 sentences with a purpose (a note, a price list, an invitation, a message) and two or three comprehension tasks. About 180 new words across the 12 units: a core N5 base, not the whole N5 list. Kanji reference pages for the unit's kanji are a separate, validated task.

Status: AI-assisted draft. Every unit needs a native review of naturalness, register and answer keys before it counts as reviewed.
