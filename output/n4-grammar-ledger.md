2026-10-05. AI-assisted author review. This records authored exposure and selected direct practice, not mastery or a complete official JLPT grammar specification. Source sequence: GENKI third-edition publisher syllabus, lessons 13–23; unit 12 is original consolidation. Vocabulary examples are not independent scored grammar tests.

- **u01 / L13:** potential forms, frequency, connected reasons し, visible そう, trying てみる, contextual なら. Direct tasks: x-can, x-frequency, x-reasons, x-bus; relevant replies/order. なら and appearance need additional independent retrieval in the expanded checkpoint bank.
- **u02 / L14:** desire ほしい, uncertainty かもしれない, gift viewpoints あげる/くれる/もらう, suggestion たらどう, number も/しか. Direct tasks: x-receive, x-shika and constrained replies/order. Giving-viewpoint alternatives and number も need broader bank coverage.
- **u03 / L15:** volitional, volitional + と思っている, ておく, modifying nouns with clauses. Direct tasks: x-volitional, x-preparation, x-order and contextual replies. Intention versus spontaneous volition needs additional independent retrieval.
- **u04 / L16:** action-benefit viewpoints, ていただけませんか, といい, 時, てすみませんでした. Direct tasks: x-request, x-received, x-wish, x-sorry, x-time. Actor/viewpoint examples remain restrained; native review is still needed.
- **u05 / L17:** hearsay そう, quotation って, たら, なくてもいい, resemblance みたい, 前に/てから. Direct tasks: x-report, x-permit, x-sequence, x-before, x-mitai and replies. Quotation and condition recognition in scenes are not full production evidence.
- **u06 / L18:** transitivity pairs, resulting-state ている, てしまう and contractions, regular と, ながら, regret ばよかった. Direct tasks: x-state, x-action, x-regret, x-auto, x-contract and x-order. No assumption that an off light is broken.
- **u07 / L19:** special honorific verbs, general respectful action/advice, てくれてありがとう, てよかった, はず. Direct tasks: x-present, x-pattern, x-host/x-friend, x-expect and contextual replies/order. Listener and actor remain explicit.
- **u08 / L20:** extra-modest special forms, general humble action patterns and special forms, ないで, embedded question/かどうか, naming という, ease やすい/にくい. Direct tasks: x-intro, x-whether, x-ease, x-without, x-register, x-reply-view, x-order. General humble forms, naming and difficulty need additional bank retrieval; mere notes/examples do not close that gap.
- **u09 / L21:** passive including adverse impact, prepared-state てある, interval 間に, adjective + する, action-wish てほしい. Recognition extensions: てる/でる, とく/どく, なきゃ. Direct tasks: x-passive, x-affected, x-ready, x-bright, x-wish, x-order, x-contract. Contracted ongoing state and obligation need additional bank coverage beyond distinguishing the three choices.
- **u10 / L22:** causative, all three causative-benefit viewpoints, なさい, ば, のに, noun resemblance のように/のような. Direct tasks: x-cause, x-condition, x-model, x-contrast, x-order, x-register. あげる/くれる contrasts and なさい currently have labelled examples; add independent retrieval to the bank rather than treating permission tasks as assessing every benefit viewpoint.
- **u11 / L23:** causative-passive, concession ても, decisions ことにする, maintained rules ことにしている, endpoint まで, method noun ます-stem + 方 (かた). Direct tasks: x-made, x-even, x-decision, x-reply-rest, x-until, x-order. Comparison 方 (ほう) is revision and does not replace the method noun.
- **u12 / review:** request actor, embedded timing question, deadlines までに, deliberately prepared states, planned versus completed action. Direct tasks: x-register, x-question, x-deadline, x-state, x-choice, x-reply-rule, x-order. Report and key have separate explicit destinations/deadlines.
